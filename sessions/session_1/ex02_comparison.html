Session 01 · Exercise 2 · 2026-11-11 · Same question, same role, two models.

The test

Question: “How long would it take to build a login page with email and password?”

  • Level 1: the question only (no role).
  • Level 2: the same question, with the role “senior software estimation expert” and rules (optimistic and pessimistic hours, task list, assumptions).
  • Models: gpt-4o-mini (OpenAI) and claude-haiku-4-5 (Anthropic).

5. What I learned

  • A role makes the answer better. Both models gave a clearer, more useful answer with the role.
  • A role costs a little more. +22% to +42%, mostly because the answer is longer.
  • The role controls the format, not the numbers. The two models gave very different hours.
  • An estimate needs context. Without the technology, the scope, and the team, the hours are not reliable. Put this context in the prompt.
  • Always check the answer. Both models made small errors that are easy to miss.
